Industry Background

The challenge

Weaning is the single most expensive intake crisis in pig production: the piglet loses the sow, the milk and the flavor signal it has imprinted on — all on the same day. In hot, humid barns the drop is steeper and lasts longer.

The formulation answer is continuity: keep the sensory world of the piglet stable while the diet underneath changes. That means a sweet, milky flavor signal in the sow feed, the creep feed and the nursery diet — identical across all three.

Customer Needs

What formulators ask us for

  • Shorten the post-weaning growth check without medicated intake stimulants
  • One flavor system usable across sow, creep and nursery feeds
  • Sweetness that survives 85°C pelleting and tropical storage
  • Verifiable trial results before committing the full program
