Industry Background

The challenge

Aqua feed energy density keeps climbing — and with it, oil inclusion. But fish digest added oil inefficiently, especially at high inclusion and in cooler water: undigested fat is FCR you paid for and excreted.

Meanwhile the attractant profile of the feed decides how much of the ration is eaten before pellets sink and leach. Intake speed and fat utilization together set your real FCR.

Customer Needs

What formulators ask us for

  • Better fat digestion as oil inclusion rises past 6–8%
  • Faster strike and consumption before nutrient leaching
  • Stable performance in tropical pond temperatures
  • A trial design that isolates emulsifier and attractant effects
