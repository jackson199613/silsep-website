Industry Background

The challenge

Above 30°C, broilers eat less — and every lost gram of intake is lost gain. At the same time, heat accelerates fat oxidation in the feed itself, so what the bird does eat is less palatable and less nutritious by the week.

The answer works on both sides: make the feed more attractive to a heat-stressed bird, and stop the feed from degrading in the silo and the bag.

Customer Needs

What formulators ask us for

  • Maintain intake through the hot season without reformulating energy
  • Prevent rancid off-notes that suppress feeding
  • Feed quality that holds through weeks of on-farm storage
  • Simple dosing compatible with existing premix programs
