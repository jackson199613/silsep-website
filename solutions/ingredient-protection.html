Industry Background

The challenge

Fishmeal and meat-bone meal are the most oxidation-prone ingredients in the feed chain — high fat, high iron, often stored for months in hot, humid warehouses. Oxidation burns energy value, destroys palatability, and in extreme cases self-heats.

Premixes face a parallel problem: vitamins and fats degrade together as trace minerals catalyze oxidation. Protection has to go in at the ingredient, not just the final feed.

Customer Needs

What formulators ask us for

  • Hold fishmeal quality through 2–3 months of tropical storage
  • Protect premix vitamin and fat value against mineral catalysis
  • Dosage flexibility from complete feed to raw material rates
  • Documentation for every batch
