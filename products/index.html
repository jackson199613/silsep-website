Product Center

Six functional additives, one engineering philosophy: palatability, preservation and bio-efficiency that survive extreme climates.

Feed Sweetener VS7188 · SweetFlow™

Carrier-free micro-granulated sweetener with 76,000,000 particles per gram — uniform sweetness in every pellet at high pelleting speeds, complete dissolution in 3 seconds, stable through 85°C conditioning. The intake driver for piglet starter, creep feed and water-line dosing.

Learn More

Feed Palatant VS1437 · AromaBoost™

Biomimetic marine attractant profile that restores the feeding signal lost when fishmeal gives way to plant proteins. Oxidation-stabilized for 180-day palatability in high-heat, high-humidity storage — validated in aqua and swine formulas.

Learn More

LPC Feed Emulsifier

Lysophospholipid biosurfactant at HLB 8–12, working exactly where digestion happens: emulsifying dietary fat into absorbable micelles in the gut. More of your added oil becomes growth — the FCR lever for high-energy aqua and young-animal feeds.

Learn More

Feed Sweetener VS3965

High-intensity blended sweetener with a clean, sucrose-like taste curve — fast onset, no bitter aftertaste, lasting sweetness. Stable in storage and through high-temperature pelleting at just 60–180 g/T in complete feed.

Learn More

Milk Flavor Palatant VS382

The classic sow-to-piglet continuity palatant: sweet fresh-milk aroma built from natural cream derivatives, lactones and MCT/SCFA fatty acids that support gut health. Trial-proven +13.65% intake and +10.58% daily gain in weaned piglets.

Learn More

Feed Antioxidant VSW5

Synergistic TBHQ + BHT + chelator system that interrupts fat oxidation at two points — protection from complete feed (100–300 g/T) up to fishmeal and meat-bone meal (2,000–3,000 g/T), with a pleasant aroma and no bitter off-note.
