Feed Sweetener
VS7188 · SweetFlow™

Carrier-free sweetness that survives 85°C pelleting — uniform in every pellet, dissolved in 3 seconds.

Overview

Buy active units, not weight

Conventional sweeteners dilute actives onto mineral carriers — you pay freight and dosing cost for filler. VS7188 SweetFlow™ is built on IAS carrier-free micro-granulation: 100% active content at 76,000,000 micro-particles per gram, a FAMI-QS certified feed safety & quality management system with batch-level traceability.

Product Advantages

Why formulators choose it

  • 76,000,000 micro-particles per gram — uniform sweetness in every pellet at high pelleting speeds
  • Carrier-free: 100% active content cuts real dosing cost 20–30% versus carrier-based grades
  • Tolerates 85°C+ conditioning without loss of sweetness
  • Complete dissolution in 3 seconds — ideal for drinking water and milk replacer applications
  • FAMI-QS certified, full batch traceability
How It Works

The principle

Sweet taste receptors respond to concentration at the taste bud, not to the average inclusion rate in the formula. Carrier-free micro-granulation multiplies the number of active particles per gram — at 76 million particles, every mouthful of feed carries a consistent sweet signal. And because there is no insoluble carrier, the full dose dissolves in seconds in water systems.
