Palatability, preservation and bio-efficiency that survive 85°C pelleting and months of tropical humidity. FAMI-QS certified production.

The Range

Buy active units, not weight

Conventional additives dilute actives onto mineral carriers. SILSEP feed additives are carrier-free: 100% active content, uniform distribution at high pelleting speeds, and a real dosing cost 20–30% lower than carrier-based grades.

Sweetener

VS7188 · SweetFlow™

Carrier-free micro-granular sweetener — 76,000,000 particles per gram for uniform sweetness in every pellet. Dissolves completely in 3 seconds, ideal for drinking water and milk replacer applications. Tolerates 85°C+ conditioning.

Palatant

VS1437 · AromaBoost™

Biomimetic flavor engineered to mask fishmeal substitutes and anti-nutritional off-notes in alternative-protein formulas. Oxidation-stabilized for 180-day palatability in high-heat, high-humidity storage.

Emulsifier

LPC Emulsifier

Lysophospholipid-based emulsifier with HLB 8–12 — engineered for high-energy aqua feeds, improving fat utilization and FCR as oil inclusion rises. (Grade code to be assigned.)

Why It Matters

Built for tropical feed mills

  • Heat resilience — stable through 85°C+ conditioning and high-speed pelleting without loss of sweetness or aroma
  • Humidity resilience — anti-oxidation and flavor-stability engineering validated for Southeast Asian and MENA climates; 180-day effective shelf life
  • Bio-efficiency for substitute ingredients — supports rising inclusion of alternative proteins and oils while maintaining intake; field results show 5–10% feed-intake uplift under heat stress
  • FAMI-QS certified — full feed-safety compliance and batch traceability
