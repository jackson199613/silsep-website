Fumed Silica
VS150 · VS200 · VS300 · VS380

Rheology control that keeps pigments suspended, films tough and vertical surfaces drip-free.

Overview

Four surface areas, one job: control

SILSEP fumed silica is added to paints and coatings for rheological control — preventing sedimentation and pigment delamination, improving weathering and scratch resistance, and strengthening the bond between coating and substrate. White powder, 10 kg paper bag.

  • Wall coatings — anti-sagging and surface smoothness; improves scratch resistance, aging performance, film hardness and self-cleaning of exterior walls
  • Powder coatings — forms a mobile surface layer that prevents moisture pickup and agglomeration, improving powder flow
  • Floor paint — better weather resistance, scratch resistance and leveling
