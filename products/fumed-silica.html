Fumed Silica
VS150 · VS200 · VS300 · VS380

Four surface areas, one job: rheology control that keeps pigments suspended and vertical films in place.

Overview

Nano-structured SiO₂ for flow you can control

SILSEP fumed silica is produced by flame hydrolysis, giving chain-like nano aggregates with very high purity (SiO₂ ≥99.9%) and BET surface areas from 150 to 380 m²/g. White powder, 10 kg paper bag.

Product Advantages

Why formulators choose it

  • Effective anti-settling and anti-sagging at low dosage
  • Prevents pigment sedimentation and delamination in storage
  • Improves weathering, scratch resistance and coating–substrate bonding
  • Restores flow in powder coatings by preventing moisture pickup and caking
  • Improves leveling, weather and scratch resistance of floor paints
How It Works

The principle

In liquid systems, fumed silica aggregates connect through hydrogen bonds into a loose three-dimensional network that holds pigments in suspension and resists sagging. Under shear — stirring, pumping, spraying — the network breaks and viscosity drops; at rest it rebuilds within seconds. This reversible thixotropy is what lets a coating spray easily yet stay put on a vertical wall.
