Milk Flavor Palatant
VS382

The classic sow-to-piglet palatant — sweet fresh-milk aroma with a fatty-acid system that supports gut health.

Overview

A proven classic, validated in trials

VS382 is a trial-proven palatant with a distinctive sweet, creamy fresh-milk aroma that strongly attracts feeding and lifts intake. Built from natural enzymatic cream derivatives, imported lactones, medium/short-chain fatty acids, ethyl vanillin and ethyl maltol on a silica flow system. Pinkish-white free-flowing powder; 540-day shelf life.

Product Advantages

Why formulators choose it

  • Distinctive sweet-milk fresh aroma — strong feeding attraction, higher intake
  • Medium-, long- and short-chain fatty acid combination benefits intestinal health
  • Maintains intake and productive performance under stress conditions
  • The choice for sow–piglet flavor continuity and weaned-piglet programs
  • Rich aroma at low inclusion; natural, pure profile
How It Works

The principle

Piglets imprint on the flavor of sow milk. VS382 reproduces that sweet dairy signal in feed — sow-to-piglet flavor continuity that reduces weaning refusal and stress-related intake drops. Its fatty-acid combination goes beyond flavor: medium- and short-chain fatty acids provide readily absorbed energy and support the intestinal environment during the critical post-weaning window.
