How It Works

The principle

Emulsifier molecules carry a hydrophilic head and a lipophilic tail. At the oil–water interface they align to lower interfacial tension and form a protective film around each droplet, preventing coalescence. The HLB value determines whether a grade favors oil-in-water (high HLB, e.g. T80) or water-in-oil (low HLB, e.g. Span 80) — and blending grades tunes the system to your exact oil phase, water quality and process conditions.
