Vista Industrial Emulsifiers
VS Series

Oil-phase and nonionic emulsifiers that make industrial emulsions more stable — and your supply chain more controllable. A verified second source for formulators.

Overview

More than a product — an introduction pathway

Vista doesn’t just ship an emulsifier. For each application we provide a comparable selection route (“lead + balance + backup”, 2–3 samples), parallel testing against your current product, manufacturer documentation (TDS, SDS, COA, batch data), and a step-by-step path from lab validation to qualified second-source supply.

  • Batch consistency — manufacturer-backed batch traceability to eliminate the lot-to-lot variation that causes emulsion splitting and rework
  • Second-source logic — qualify us alongside your current supplier first; replace nothing until parallel tests prove it
  • Total-formula costing — compare by actual dosage and delivered cost per tonne of formula, not price per kilogram
  • Compliance first — product, importer, tonnage and end use confirmed before documentation is issued

Solutions

Four application routes

Metalworking

Stable O/W metalworking fluids

For semi-synthetic and emulsion-type metalworking fluids, soluble cutting fluid concentrates and industrial cleaning systems. Route: VS-210 or VS-610 as lead, VS-410 / VS-620 for balance, VS-420 for oil-phase adjustment. Solves dilution instability, splitting, foam and the lubricity–stability trade-off.

Release & Lubrication

Oil-phase stability & industrial release

For oil-based release agents, cutting oils and oil-phase treatment fluids. Route: VS-201A as lead, VS-420 for compatibility, VS-201B and VS-410 as alternatives. Solves oil-phase splitting, cloudiness, uneven spreading and release residue.

Textile & Leather

Wetting, softening & antistatic

For spin finishes, textile softeners, finishing auxiliaries and leather fatliquors. Route: VS-410 as lead, VS-610 / VS-620 for waterborne routes, VS-430 for antistatic and smoothness, VS-201A for oil-phase affinity. Solves uneven oiling, emulsion instability and hand-feel variation.

Oilfield

Drilling-fluid emulsifier validation

For regional oilfield service companies and drilling-fluid formulators: a cautious lab-first pathway using VS-201A, VS-420 and custom blends — emulsion stability, rheology, fluid loss and contamination tolerance verified before any field-scale introduction.

How We Work

From sample to qualified supplier

  • 1. Application diagnosis — product type, oil/water system, current grade, target performance, annual volume, destination country
  • 2. Sample routes — a focused “lead + balance + backup” selection, never the whole catalog
  • 3. Parallel testing — benchmarked against your current product: storage, dilution, foam, lubricity, wetting, hand-feel
  • 4. Documentation & support — TDS, SDS, COA, batch data and manufacturer technical coordination
  • 5. Trial order → long-term supply — qualify as second source first, then grow into regular procurement
