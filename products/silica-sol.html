Silica Sol

Aqueous colloidal silica dispersions with controlled particle size and stability — the binder and modifier modern processes rely on.

Overview

Colloidal silica, engineered for stability

SILSEP silica sol is an aqueous dispersion of nano-scale colloidal SiO₂ particles with tight particle-size distribution and long shelf stability. Available in different particle sizes, concentrations and surface modifications to match your process. Detailed grades and specifications available on request.

  • High binding strength for investment casting shells and refractory systems
  • Improves hardness, anti-slip and anti-soil performance in coatings
  • Stable in a wide pH window; low sodium grades available
  • Uniform nano particle size for consistent film formation
Applications

Where silica sol performs

Precision investment casting

Shell binder with high green strength and excellent surface reproduction.

Coatings & inorganic binders

Hardness, adhesion and heat resistance for inorganic and hybrid coatings.

Catalyst carriers & textiles

Carrier binder for catalysts; anti-slip and finishing agent for textiles.
