Silica Sol

Aqueous colloidal silica with controlled particle size and stability — the binder modern processes rely on.

Overview

Colloidal silica, engineered for stability

SILSEP silica sol is an aqueous dispersion of nano-scale colloidal SiO₂ particles with tight particle-size distribution and long shelf stability. Available in different particle sizes, concentrations and surface modifications. Detailed grades and specifications on request.

Product Advantages

Why formulators choose it

  • High binding strength for investment casting shells and refractory systems
  • Improves hardness, anti-slip and anti-soil performance of coatings
  • Stable across a wide pH window; low-sodium grades available
  • Uniform nano particle size for consistent film formation
How It Works

The principle

Silica sol particles carry a charged surface layer that keeps them dispersed indefinitely in water. When water is removed — by drying or gelation — the particles pack and form siloxane bonds with each other and with the substrate, creating a hard, heat-resistant inorganic network. That transition from stable liquid to ceramic-like binder is what makes silica sol the backbone of precision casting shells and inorganic coatings.
