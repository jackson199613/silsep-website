Silica Anti-Blocking
Agent

Controlled-particle silica that keeps film layers separating cleanly — without sacrificing clarity.

Overview

Slip and separation for high-clarity films

In film production, smooth adjacent layers cling together (blocking), causing breaks and waste at winding and converting speed. SILSEP anti-blocking silica creates a controlled micro-spacing between layers while its refractive index keeps haze to a minimum.

Product Advantages

Why formulators choose it

  • Precisely classified particle size for reliable layer separation
  • High transparency — minimal haze penalty in optical and packaging films
  • Narrow distribution: no oversize particles that scratch or pinhole thin films
  • Excellent dispersion in PE, PP, PET and coating masterbatch
  • Synergy with slip additives for lower COF
How It Works

The principle

Blocking occurs when two smooth film surfaces come into full contact and adhere through van der Waals forces. Anti-blocking silica particles protrude slightly from the film surface, creating micro-asperities that hold the surfaces apart and reduce the true contact area — layers release cleanly at winding, bag-opening and converting, while particle size below the visible threshold preserves optical clarity.
