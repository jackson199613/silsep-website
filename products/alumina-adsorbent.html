Alumina Printing
Media Adsorbent

High-purity alumina and alumina sol for premium glossy inkjet media — photographic color with instant-dry handling.

Overview

The gloss layer behind photo-grade prints

SILSEP alumina adsorbents form the ink-receiving layer of premium glossy inkjet papers and films. High-purity boehmite with controlled crystal form delivers gloss, transparency and rapid ink fixation. Grades and coating guidance on request.

Product Advantages

Why formulators choose it

  • High-purity boehmite / alumina sol with controlled crystal form
  • Rapid ink fixation for sharp dots and zero bleeding
  • High gloss and transparency for photographic media
  • Consistent viscosity and coating behavior at production speed
How It Works

The principle

Unlike silica, boehmite alumina surfaces carry a positive charge in coating conditions. Anionic dye and pigment inks are electrostatically fixed the instant they touch the layer, while the liquid vehicle drains into the pore structure. The combination — instant fixation on top, fast drainage below — is what produces the high-gloss, high-density image quality of photo-grade media.
