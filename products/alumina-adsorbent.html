Alumina Printing
Media Adsorbent

High-purity alumina and alumina sol for premium glossy inkjet media — fast ink absorption with photographic color depth.

Overview

The gloss layer behind photo-grade prints

SILSEP alumina adsorbents form the ink-receiving layer of premium glossy inkjet papers and films. Positive surface charge fixes dye and pigment inks rapidly, giving sharp dots, vivid color and instant dry handling. Grades and coating guidance available on request.

  • High-purity boehmite / alumina sol with controlled crystal form
  • Rapid ink fixation for sharp dot definition and no bleeding
  • High gloss and transparency for photographic media
  • Consistent viscosity and coating behavior at production speed
Applications

Where it performs

Photo-grade inkjet paper

Cast-coated and RC glossy papers with instant-dry handling.

Wide-format display media

Vivid color reproduction for advertising and display films.

Specialty coated films

Ink-receiving layers on synthetic and transparent media.
