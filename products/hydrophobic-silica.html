Hydrophobic Silica
VS17

Chemically bonded silane modification for systems where moisture is the enemy.

Overview

Surface-treated for polar systems

VS17 is produced by reacting hydrophilic silica with active silanes (chlorosilane / HMDS types), chemically bonding the treatment to the particle surface. SiO₂ ≥95%, BET ≥160 m²/g, particle size 1–45 μm, bulk density 120–140 g/L. White powder, 10 kg paper bag.

Product Advantages

Why formulators choose it

  • Low moisture absorption — performance holds in humid processing and storage
  • Excellent dispersion, even in polar liquids such as epoxy resins
  • Rheology adjustment capability in both polar and non-polar systems
  • Structural modification available on top of hydrophobic treatment for custom development
  • Improves dielectric properties and corrosion resistance of filled systems
How It Works

The principle

Untreated silica carries surface silanol groups that attract water, causing viscosity drift and caking. In VS17 those silanols are chemically capped with silane groups: the particle surface becomes permanently water-repellent while retaining its nano-structure. The result — stable thickening in polar resins, low moisture pickup, and reliable powder flow even at high humidity.
