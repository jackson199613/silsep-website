Silica Pharma
Excipient

Pharmaceutical-grade silicon dioxide for flow, stability and carrier functions in solid dosage forms.

Overview

Purity and function for solid dosage forms

SILSEP pharmaceutical-grade silica serves as glidant, anti-caking agent, carrier and stabilizer in tablets, capsules and nutraceutical formulations. Produced under strict quality management with full batch traceability. Compendial compliance documentation on request.

Product Advantages

Why formulators choose it

  • Improves powder flow and content uniformity in high-speed tableting
  • High oil-absorption grades for liquisolid and oil-carrier systems
  • Anti-caking protection for hygroscopic actives and premixes
  • Full batch traceability and quality documentation
How It Works

The principle

Nano-scale silica particles coat the surface of larger powder grains, acting as spacers that reduce inter-particle friction and moisture bridging. Flow improves, hygroscopic blends stop caking, and die filling becomes consistent shot after shot. High-porosity grades go further — adsorbing liquid actives and oils into a free-flowing powder that can be tableted or encapsulated.
