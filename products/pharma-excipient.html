Silica Pharma
Excipient

Pharmaceutical-grade silicon dioxide for flow, stability and carrier functions in solid dosage forms.

Overview

Purity and function for solid dosage forms

SILSEP pharmaceutical-grade silica serves as glidant, anti-caking agent, carrier and stabilizer in tablets, capsules and nutraceutical formulations. Produced under strict quality management with full batch traceability. Compendial compliance details and documentation available on request.

  • Improves powder flow and content uniformity in high-speed tableting
  • High oil-absorption grades for liquisolid and oil-carrier systems
  • Anti-caking protection for hygroscopic actives and premixes
  • Full batch traceability and quality documentation
Applications

Where it functions

Tablets & capsules

Glidant and anti-adherent for consistent die filling and ejection.

Nutraceuticals

Carrier for oils and actives; stability for hygroscopic blends.

Premixes & powders

Free-flowing, caking-free powder blends with uniform dosing.
