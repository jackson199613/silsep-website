Silica Anti-Rust
Pigment

Eco-friendly, heavy-metal-free anticorrosive pigments for modern protective coating systems.

Overview

Corrosion protection without heavy metals

SILSEP anti-rust pigments use ion-exchanged silica technology to deliver active corrosion inhibition without zinc chromate or other heavy metals — meeting the environmental requirements of modern protective coatings. Grades and dosage guidance on request.

Product Advantages

Why formulators choose it

  • Heavy-metal-free, environmentally compliant corrosion inhibition
  • Compatible with waterborne and solventborne binder systems
  • Active inhibition at the coating–metal interface, beyond barrier effect
  • Low dosage with minimal impact on gloss and rheology
How It Works

The principle

The pigment consists of silica particles ion-exchanged with inhibiting cations. When moisture and corrosive ions penetrate the film, an ion-exchange reaction captures aggressive species and releases inhibiting ions exactly where corrosion would start — the coating–metal interface — forming a passivating layer on the steel. Protection is active and self-directing, not just a physical barrier.
