Silica Anti-Rust Pigment

Eco-friendly, heavy-metal-free anticorrosive pigments engineered for modern protective coating systems.

Overview

Corrosion protection without heavy metals

SILSEP anti-rust pigments use ion-exchanged silica technology to deliver active corrosion inhibition without zinc chromate or other heavy metals — meeting the environmental requirements of modern protective coatings. Grades and dosage guidance available on request.

  • Heavy-metal-free, environmentally compliant corrosion inhibition
  • Compatible with waterborne and solventborne systems
  • Active inhibition at the coating–metal interface, not just barrier effect
  • Low dosage, minimal impact on gloss and rheology
Applications

Where it protects

Industrial protective coatings

Primer systems for steel structures, machinery and marine environments.

Coil & container coatings

Thin-film corrosion protection where dosage efficiency matters.

Waterborne systems

Stable inhibition performance in modern low-VOC formulations.
