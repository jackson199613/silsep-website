Feed Palatant
VS1437 · AromaBoost™

Biomimetic flavor and lysophospholipid emulsification for alternative-protein formulas in tropical climates.

Overview

Palatability engineered for substitution

As fishmeal and fish oil give way to plant proteins and alternative oils across Southeast Asia, South Asia and MENA, feed palatability and fat utilization become the limiting factors. SILSEP pairs a biomimetic palatant with lysophospholipid emulsification to keep intake and FCR on target. FAMI-QS certified.

Product Advantages

Why formulators choose it

  • Biomimetic aroma engineered to mask off-notes of fishmeal substitutes and plant proteins
  • Oxidation-stabilized for 180-day palatability in high-heat, high-humidity storage
  • LPC emulsifier with HLB 8–12 improves fat digestion as oil inclusion rises
  • Field results: 5–10% feed-intake uplift under tropical heat stress
  • Stable through 85°C+ conditioning and high-speed pelleting
How It Works

The principle

Aquatic and young animals select feed by chemosensory cues within seconds of contact. VS1437 reproduces the key attractant molecules of marine raw materials — restoring the feeding signal that substitution removes — while its antioxidant system protects those volatile actives through months of tropical storage. In parallel, lysophospholipids (HLB 8–12) act as biosurfactants in the gut, emulsifying fat into micelles small enough for efficient absorption.
