Silica Matting Agent
VS Series

Amorphous ultra-fine silica engineered for precise gloss control — without sacrificing transparency, dispersibility or film properties.

Overview

Engineered SiO₂·nH₂O for coatings that need to look right

SILSEP matting agents are synthetic amorphous silica in white powder form with porous structure, excellent optical properties and tight particle-size control (2–13 μm). All grades: whiteness 98, SiO₂ (dry basis) ≥99.9%, pH 6.5–7.5. Packaging: 10 kg paper bag.

Product Advantages

Why formulators choose it

  • Rich particle-size portfolio to match different film effects and gloss targets
  • Refractive index close to most coating resins — matting without haze or loss of color depth
  • Low reliance on anti-settling agents; efficient gloss retention in storage
  • Balanced matting and smooth surface feel; excellent scratch resistance, less burnishing
  • Outstanding dispersibility and anti-blocking behavior
  • Suitable for both waterborne and solventborne systems
How It Works

The principle

After a coating containing matting agent is applied, silica particles distribute evenly through the wet film. As solvent evaporates the film shrinks; the matting particles shrink more slowly than the film, creating a micro-rough surface. Incident light is diffusely reflected by this micro-texture instead of mirrored — producing a uniform matte effect. Particle size and pore structure determine the balance between matting efficiency, transparency and feel.
