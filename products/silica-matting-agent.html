Silica Matting Agent
VS Series

Precise gloss control without sacrificing transparency, dispersibility or film properties — 11 grades engineered by system and cure chemistry.

Overview

Engineered SiO₂·nH₂O for coatings that need to look right

SILSEP matting agents are synthetic amorphous silica (SiO₂·nH₂O) in white powder form, produced with tight particle-size and pore-structure control. Standard packaging: 10 kg paper bag.

  • High transparency — matting without haze, preserving color depth in clear and pigmented systems
  • High matting efficiency — lower dosage for target gloss, from deep-matte furniture finishes to soft-touch plastic coatings
  • Excellent suspension & dispersibility — easy incorporation, stable in storage, no hard settling
  • System-matched grades — dedicated grades for UV cure (VS370U), coil (VS3651C), leather (VS331P) and waterborne systems (VS370T)
