LPC Feed
Emulsifier

Lysophospholipid emulsification at HLB 8–12 — turning dietary fat into absorbable energy as oil inclusion rises.

Overview

Bio-efficiency for high-energy formulas

As energy density rises in modern aqua and livestock feeds, fat digestion becomes the bottleneck — especially in young animals with immature bile systems. SILSEP LPC is a lysophospholipid-based biosurfactant engineered to emulsify dietary fat into micelles small enough for efficient absorption. FAMI-QS certified production.

Product Advantages

Why formulators choose it

  • HLB 8–12 — the optimal hydrophilic–lipophilic balance for gut-phase fat emulsification
  • Improves fat utilization and FCR as oil inclusion rises in high-energy aqua feeds
  • Supports young animals whose natural bile secretion is not yet mature
  • Enables reformulation: maintain energy performance with lower added oil
  • Heat-stable through pelleting; consistent batch-to-batch performance
How It Works

The principle

Fat digestion requires emulsification: large fat globules must be broken into micro-droplets before lipase can work and micelles can carry fatty acids across the gut wall. Lysophospholipids are stronger emulsifiers than ordinary lecithin because their single fatty-acid tail packs tighter at the oil–water interface, forming smaller, more stable micelles at gut pH. At HLB 8–12, LPC works exactly where digestion happens — the aqueous gut environment — converting more of your added oil into absorbed energy instead of excreted waste.
