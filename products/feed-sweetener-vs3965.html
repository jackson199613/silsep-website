Feed Sweetener
VS3965

Sucrose-like sweetness with fast diffusion and lasting profile — stable through storage heat and high-temperature pelleting.

Overview

Pure, fast, stable sweetness

VS3965 is a high-intensity blended sweetener delivering a clean, sucrose-like taste that animals accept immediately. Engineered on a neotame – sodium saccharin system with a silica flow carrier, it stays stable under conventional storage and high-temperature pelleting alike. Off-white free-flowing powder; 10 kg foil bag inner, 20/25 kg laminated bag outer; 18-month shelf life.

Product Advantages

Why formulators choose it

  • Sucrose-like taste profile — pure, comfortable sweetness with strong attraction effect
  • Fast onset: strong sweetness diffusion with long-lasting sweet perception
  • Excellent stability in storage and through high-temperature pelleting
  • Stimulates appetite and secretion of digestive enzymes and juices — measurably higher intake
  • Low moisture, high fineness, free-flowing — mixes uniformly into feed
  • High sweetness intensity at low dosage: cost reduction with long-term safety, no residue
How It Works

The principle

Sweet receptors on the animal tongue respond to sweetness intensity and onset speed, not to sugar content. VS3965 combines a fast-onset, high-intensity sweetener system tuned to mimic the taste curve of sucrose — immediate recognition, no bitter aftertaste — triggering appetite and digestive secretion. The silica flow system keeps the low-dosage active distributed uniformly through tonnes of feed.
