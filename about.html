Talk to Us

SILSEP is the animal nutrition brand of the Censil group, focused on one problem the global additive industry treats as an afterthought: performance in hot, humid climates. Our sweeteners, palatants, emulsifiers and antioxidants are engineered from the climate backwards — for 85°C+ pelleting, months of tropical storage, and the raw-material realities of fast-growing feed markets.

Core products — carrier-free sweetener VS7188, biomimetic palatant VS1437, milk flavor VS382, sweetener VS3965, LPC emulsifier and antioxidant VSW5 — serve feed mills, premix producers and integrators across Southeast Asia, South Asia, the Middle East and beyond.

We position ourselves deliberately between two worlds: the underlying process technology of the global leaders, without the brand premium — optimized for your local climate, raw materials and cost targets, and backed by FAMI-QS certified production with batch-level traceability.

Brand Culture

Every feed mill in a hot, humid market deserves additives engineered for its reality — not diluted versions of a temperate-climate formula. We build for the climate first, then for the cost sheet.

From first sample to long-term supply, we aim to be the supplier formulators call when the environment is the hardest constraint — trusted for data, honesty about limits, and consistency batch after batch.

We publish trial data, recommend parallel testing against your current product, and support introduction step by step — sample, trial, small order, then scale.

Specialization is our moat. We would rather be the definitive answer for tropical feed performance than a footnote in a global catalog.
