About SILSEP

A technology brand of Censil — precision particle engineering for customers across Asia, MENA, Europe and the Americas.

Who We Are

Specialists, not generalists

SILSEP focuses on one thing: functional particles that perform under extreme conditions. Our silica lines serve coatings, plastics, paper and ceramics formulators; our feed additive line serves feed mills operating in the world’s hottest, most humid markets. Both are built on the same core capability — precise control of particle size, surface chemistry and stability.
