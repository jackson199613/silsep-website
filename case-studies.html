Swine · Weaned Piglets

Restoring post-weaning intake with VS382 milk flavor

The need. A commercial trial farm in Sichuan, China needed to shorten the post-weaning growth check in 50-day-old piglets without medicated intake stimulants.

The solution. VS382 sweet-milk palatant added at 600 g/T to the nursery diet; control group fed the identical diet without palatant; 40 animals, randomized, 30-day trial period.

The measured result.

+13.65%

daily feed intake vs control

+10.58%

daily weight gain

+3.53%

feed conversion (G/F)
