REGULATORY POSITION BY SUBSTANCE AND MARKET

Most supplier compliance pages list certificates and stop. That tells you what a company holds, not whether its product can enter your market. This page sets out the substance-level position for the three regulated actives in our range, market by market, with the primary instrument linked rather than summarised — including the cases where the answer is no.

How to read this page

  • Where we could verify a position in a primary source, it is stated and linked.
  • Where we could not, the cell says not verified — that means we did not find it, not that it is permitted.
  • Nothing here is a customs guarantee. It is the factual basis for your own determination.

2. TBHQ — affects VSW5

MarketPositionBasis
ChinaPermitted — antioxidant category, farmed animals MARA feed additive catalogue
European Union Not authorised for feed. The authorised feed antioxidants are BHA (E 320), BHT (E 321) and propyl gallate (E 310) EU Register of Feed Additives under Reg. (EC) 1831/2003
United States Not in the feed provisions. TBHQ appears in 21 CFR under human food only 21 CFR 172.185 (Subchapter B); no entry in the animal feed subchapter
Southeast Asia, Middle East, North Africa Not verified—

Plainly: VSW5 is not the right product for EU or US feed. If that is your market you need a BHA/BHT/propyl gallate system or a natural one, and we would rather tell you now.

3. Ethoxyquin — we do not use it

VSW5 contains no ethoxyquin. This is worth stating because the regulatory position has moved and some suppliers still describe it as merely suspended:
