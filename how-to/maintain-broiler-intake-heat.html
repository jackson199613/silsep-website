How to Maintain Broiler Intake in Hot Season

Above 30°C, broilers trade eating for heat dissipation. You cannot air-condition your way out on most farms — but you can make every bite count and every bite appealing.

Steps

  1. Shift feeding to cool hours. Front-load feed access to early morning and late evening; withdraw during peak heat to avoid metabolic heat load from digestion.
  2. Protect feed freshness. Heat accelerates fat oxidation and rancid feed suppresses intake further. VSW5 at 100-300 g/T in complete feed holds palatability through the hot chain.
  3. Add an appetite signal. A sucrose-like sweetener (VS3965, 60-180 g/T) stimulates appetite and digestive secretion — field programs in Southeast Asia target the 5-10% intake recovery band.
  4. Watch water first. Hot birds drink before they eat; cool, clean water at adequate flow is the precondition for any feed-side measure.
  5. Track and adjust weekly. Plot daily intake against house temperature; if the gap persists after two weeks, review stocking density and ventilation before adding more additive.

Common mistakes

  • Raising energy density without emulsification or freshness protection — oxidized fat undoes the gain
  • Changing three things at once, learning nothing
  • Treating additives as a substitute for ventilation basics
