Sustainability

Efficiency is our environmental strategy: every gram of filler we don’t ship, and every tonne of feed that doesn’t spoil, is impact avoided.

Environment

Less filler, less waste, less freight

Carrier-free micro-granulation means customers ship and dose active units instead of mineral filler — cutting transport emissions and inert material entering the feed chain by 20–30% versus carrier-based grades. Our antioxidant systems extend the usable life of feed and raw materials in tropical storage, directly reducing spoilage waste at the mill and the farm.

Social

Food security where it matters most

SILSEP products serve feed mills in fast-growing markets across Southeast Asia, South Asia and MENA — where efficient animal protein production is a food-security priority. By helping local mills reach the performance of global-brand formulas at accessible cost, we support the competitiveness of regional producers and the affordability of protein for their markets.

Governance & Quality

Compliance before commerce

Production is FAMI-QS certified with batch-level traceability from raw material to shipment. We confirm product classification, importer, tonnage and end use before issuing documentation — and we do not make claims a certificate cannot back. Quality documentation (TDS, SDS, COA) accompanies every commercial engagement.
