Sustainability

SUSTAINABLE VALUE FOR THE FEED CHAIN

EnvironmentSocietyGovernance

Creating sustainable value where protein demand grows fastest

Sustainability at SILSEP is not a report — it is the product philosophy itself. Every gram of filler we do not ship, every tonne of feed that does not spoil, and every formula that converts more of its energy into growth is environmental impact avoided. We pursue this through three commitments: efficiency in what we make, value for the communities we serve, and compliance in how we operate.

Toward a Greener Feed Chain Environment

Our environmental strategy is efficiency. Carrier-free concentration, oxidation control and optimized logistics reduce material, waste and freight across the feed chain — effects that scale with every tonne our customers produce.

At 100% active content, our carrier-free products eliminate the mineral filler that makes up the majority of conventional additive volume — less mass manufactured, packed, shipped and added to feed.

VSW5 antioxidant protection extends the usable life of feed, fishmeal and premixes in tropical storage — preventing spoilage losses at the mill and the farm, where feed waste is both an economic and an environmental cost.

Concentrated products mean fewer containers per active unit delivered. Export consolidation and full-container planning keep freight emissions per tonne of performance as low as practical.

Concentration is conservation

One tonne of carrier-free VS7188 delivers the active units of roughly 2.5 tonnes of a conventional 40%-active carrier grade — the difference is packaging, freight and inert filler that never need to exist.

Creating Social Value Society

SILSEP serves markets where efficient animal protein production is a food-security priority. Our social value is measured in the competitiveness of the local mills we supply and the affordability of the protein they help produce.

By bringing global-grade additive performance to fast-growing markets without the brand premium, we help regional feed producers compete — and help keep animal protein affordable for their consumers.

We transfer method, not just product: trial protocols, dosage guidance and formulation support that leave customer teams more capable after every project.

A focused team built on technical depth and export discipline — with development paths that reward evidence, honesty and customer results.

Performance where it matters most

Field results across Southeast Asia show 5–10% feed-intake uplift under heat stress — efficiency gains that translate directly into more affordable protein in the markets that need it.

Compliance-First Governance Governance

We operate on a simple rule: compliance before commerce. Certifications are verifiable, claims match certificate scope, and documentation precedes every shipment.

FAMI-QS (CNBJ380053-CN, Site FAM-0673), ISO 22000:2018 (114FSMS2300022) and Halal (SSPY-201318-32223-R3) — every number verifiable with the issuing body. View certificates →
